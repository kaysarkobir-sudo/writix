✏️ Step 2: Fill Your Data

📝 How to Fill the Template
Required Fields:
  • name - Book title
  • author - Author name
  • price - Price in BDT (numbers only, e.g., 850.00)
  • quantity - Total number of copies
Category Options:
  • fiction, non_fiction, science, mathematics, history, geography, literature, biography, reference, other
Tips:
  • Delete the instruction row before uploading
  • Delete the sample data row
  • Leave available_quantity same as quantity for new books
  • Use consistent date format: YYYY (e.g., 2024)
